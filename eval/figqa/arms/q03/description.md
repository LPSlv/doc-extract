## p001-x2899.png

**Figure (line-art package pinout drawing).** SOT-23 style three-lead surface-mount
package shown in plan view (looking down on the body). The body is a plain
rectangle, wider than tall, drawn with a heavy black outline and no fill.

Three leads project from the body as small rectangular tabs:

- One lead at the **top edge**, positioned at the horizontal centre of the body,
  labelled **C** (label sits above the lead, outside the outline). The outer tip of
  this lead is drawn with a thickened/filled black bar.
- One lead at the **bottom-left**, labelled **B** (label below the lead).
- One lead at the **bottom-right**, labelled **E** (label below the lead).

So the pin assignment is C = single lead on the opposite side from the pair,
B = bottom-left of the pair, E = bottom-right of the pair. B, C and E are the only
strings in the image — no part number, no dimensions, no pin numbers, no scale.

## p001-x2900.png

**Figure (line-art package marking diagram).** The same SOT-23 style three-lead
package outline in plan view: rectangular body with one lead on the top edge at
the horizontal centre, and two leads on the bottom edge (bottom-left and
bottom-right). Leads are unlabelled in this image.

Two markings are printed inside the body outline:

- **`K1N`** — large upright characters, occupying the left/centre of the body. This
  is the device type marking.
- **`YM`** — smaller characters set near the right end of the body, rotated 90°
  relative to the `K1N` text and stacked vertically (reading bottom-to-top: Y then
  M). This is the date-code field.

No other text, numbers, dimensions or logos appear.
