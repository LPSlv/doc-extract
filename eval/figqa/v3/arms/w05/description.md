# Visual descriptions — w05 (4 images, all from page 8)

All four images are cropped digital-oscilloscope screen captures on a white
background with a black rectangular border. **None of the four contains any
legible text** — there are no channel labels, no volts/div or time/div readouts,
no measurement values, no cursor annotations and no title. Only the graticule,
the traces and (in two of them) a small orange trigger-position marker are
present. All positions below are therefore given in **graticule divisions**
measured from the centre of the screen, and as fractions of the plot where that
is clearer.

Every one of the four uses the same graticule: **10 horizontal divisions × 8
vertical divisions**, drawn as faint grey dotted lines, with a solid centre
vertical axis and a solid centre horizontal axis, each carrying dense fine minor
tick marks (5 minor ticks per division).

## p008-x292.png

**Figure (oscilloscope screenshot, two traces).** 340 × 274 px. Graticule
10 × 8 divisions; solid centre vertical axis at x = 169 px (0.497 of width),
solid centre horizontal axis with minor ticks at y = 137 px (0.500 of height).
Dotted vertical gridlines at x ≈ 33, 67, 101, 135, 169, 203, 237, 271, 305;
dotted horizontal gridlines at y ≈ 34, 68, 102, 136, 171, 204, 239.

Traces:

- **Blue (noisy, upper).** A flat, visibly noisy horizontal line sitting at
  **+2.13 divisions above the centre line** (y ≈ 63–65 px, i.e. 1.87 divisions
  below the top edge). It is flat across the whole 10-division sweep; the level
  is the same at the left edge, at centre and at the right edge. Trace
  thickness ~3 px (~0.09 division) of noise band. It carries one narrow
  **downward glitch at x ≈ 102–104 px (3.0 divisions from the left edge, i.e.
  2.0 divisions left of centre)**, where it dips briefly from +2.18 to +1.85
  divisions — a spike roughly 0.33 division deep and less than 0.1 division
  wide.
- **Thin pale blue / violet line.** A separate, much fainter and thinner
  straight horizontal line runs the full width at **+1.85 divisions above
  centre** (y ≈ 73–74 px), about 0.28 division below the noisy blue trace. It is
  perfectly flat, with no transitions. The blue glitch above reaches down to
  exactly this level.
- **Green (lower), a rectangular pulse.** Low level at **−1.99 divisions**
  (y ≈ 205 px), high level at **−0.75 divisions** (y ≈ 163 px), so the pulse
  amplitude is **1.24 divisions**. Both edges are essentially vertical
  (transition completes inside ~2 px, < 0.1 division). **Rising edge at
  x = 104 px = 3.06 divisions from the left edge** (2.0 divisions left of
  centre). **Falling edge at x = 267 px = 7.85 divisions from the left edge**
  (2.85 divisions right of centre). **Pulse width ≈ 4.8 divisions.** The green
  trace's own high and low segments are noisy at roughly the same level as the
  blue trace's noise.

The green rising edge and the blue trace's downward glitch occur at the same
horizontal position (3.0 divisions), i.e. they are time-coincident.

No trigger marker is visible in this image.

## p008-x307.png

**Figure (oscilloscope screenshot, two traces).** 452 × 365 px. Graticule
10 × 8 divisions; solid centre vertical axis at x = 227 px, solid centre
horizontal axis with fine minor ticks at y ≈ 182 px. Dotted vertical gridlines

Markers:

  the centre vertical axis** (x = 227 px, y ≈ 2 px) — the trigger-position
  marker.
- A second, much fainter pale-orange tick sits on the top edge at
  **x ≈ 92 px (2.04 divisions from the left edge)**.

Traces:

- **Cyan (upper).** A flat, noisy horizontal band centred on **+2.00 divisions
  above the centre line** (y ≈ 88–97 px). Within the band there is a dashed
  cyan line along the top of the noise and a thin solid cyan line along its
  bottom. The cyan trace is flat for the entire sweep — **it has no transition
  anywhere**, and its level at the left edge (+1.99 div) matches its level at
  the right edge (+1.96 div). Peak-to-peak noise ≈ 0.15 division.
- **Blue (lower), a single trapezoidal pulse** with slow, smoothly rounded
  (S-shaped, bandwidth-limited) edges.
  - Low baseline: **−0.95 divisions** (y ≈ 226 px), held from the left edge to
    the start of the rise, and again from the end of the fall to the right edge.
  - Flat top: **+0.11 divisions** (y ≈ 177 px) — i.e. the plateau lies almost
    exactly on the centre horizontal axis, just above it.
  - **Amplitude ≈ 1.06 divisions.**
  - Rising edge: 10 % at **1.33 div**, 50 % at **1.88 div**, 90 % at
    **2.54 div** from the left edge → **10–90 % rise ≈ 1.21 divisions**.
  - Falling edge: 90 % at **7.35 div**, 50 % at **7.90 div**, 10 % at
    **8.94 div** → **90–10 % fall ≈ 1.59 divisions** (the fall is noticeably
    slower than the rise, with a long tail settling towards the right edge).
  - **Pulse width at 50 % ≈ 6.0 divisions**, roughly centred on the screen
    (50 % points at 1.88 and 7.90 divisions; midpoint 4.89 divisions ≈ centre).

## p008-x345.png

**Figure (oscilloscope screenshot, three traces, step response).** 451 × 363 px.
Graticule 10 × 8 divisions; solid centre vertical axis at x = 225 px, solid
centre horizontal axis with fine minor ticks at y ≈ 181 px. Dotted vertical
gridlines at x ≈ 43, 88, 134, 179, 225, 270, 315, 361, 406; dotted horizontal
gridlines at y ≈ 44, 89, 135, 181, 226, 272, 316.

No orange trigger marker is visible in this image.

All three traces are flat before and after a single step, and **all three step
at the same horizontal position, x ≈ 110 px = 2.44 divisions from the left
edge** (2.56 divisions left of centre). All edges are essentially vertical.

- **Cyan (top trace).** Low level **+2.03 divisions above centre**
  (y ≈ 89 px); steps **up** at 2.46 divisions to a high level of **+3.31
  divisions above centre** (y ≈ 31 px), which it holds flat to the right edge.
  **Step amplitude ≈ 1.28 divisions.** The high level lies about 0.7 division
  below the top edge of the graticule.
- **Blue (middle trace).** Low level **−0.96 divisions below centre**
  (y ≈ 225 px); steps **up** at 2.44 divisions to a high level of **+1.03
  divisions above centre** (y ≈ 134 px). **Step amplitude ≈ 2.0 divisions** —
  the largest of the three, and the step carries the blue trace from one
  division below the centre axis to one division above it, crossing the centre
  horizontal axis. Immediately after the edge there is a small overshoot /
  settling notch: for roughly the first 0.2 division after the transition the
  trace sits a fraction of a division high before settling onto its flat top,
  which then holds to the right edge (+1.02 div at the right edge). Both levels
  are noisy, band thickness ~3 px.
- **Magenta / pink (bottom trace).** Flat at **−3.02 divisions below centre**
  (y ≈ 318 px) for the entire sweep — **it does not step**. Its only feature is
  a single narrow **upward glitch spike at x ≈ 110 px (2.44 divisions)**,
  time-coincident with the cyan and blue edges; the spike reaches up to about
  y ≈ 275 px, roughly 0.95 division tall, and is only 1–2 px wide. The magenta
  level is identical at the left edge (−3.02 div) and the right edge
  (−3.02 div).

Vertical separation between traces after the step: cyan high at +3.31 div, blue
high at +1.03 div, magenta at −3.02 div.
