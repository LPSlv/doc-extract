# Visual description — w20 (page 3)

Two greyscale photographs of agarose gel electrophoresis results (ethidium-
bromide-style images, dark bands on a grey background). Both are ARMS/tetra-
primer PCR genotyping gels: each sample lane carries a constant control band
plus one or two allele-specific bands, and each lane is labelled at the top with
the genotype it produced. Neither image has a printed figure number or caption
inside it. Molecular-weight-marker sizes are printed to the left of the gel; the
diagnostic band identities are printed to the right.

---

## p003-x102.png

**Photograph (agarose gel, 6 lanes).**

**Lane headings, left to right:** **M**, **T/C**, **T/T**, **C/C**, **T/T**,
**T/C**. Lane 1 (M) is the DNA size marker; lanes 2–6 are samples.

**Size-marker labels printed at the left edge (top to bottom):** **500**,
**400**, **300**, **200**, **100**. The marker lane is a regularly spaced
100 bp-type ladder showing roughly a dozen rungs, the upper ones closely packed
and the labelled 500/400/300/200/100 rungs spaced further apart lower down.

**Band identity labels printed at the right edge (top to bottom):**
- **456 bp (control band)** — aligned just below the 500 marker and above 400.
- **313 bp (C allele)** — aligned just above the 300 marker.
- **194 bp (T allele)** — aligned just below the 200 marker.

**Band pattern per lane** (✓ = band present, — = absent):

| Lane | Label | 456 bp control | 313 bp (C allele) | 194 bp (T allele) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | M | ladder | ladder | ladder |
| 2 | T/C | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| 3 | T/T | ✓ | — | ✓ |
| 4 | C/C | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| 5 | T/T | ✓ | — | ✓ |
| 6 | T/C | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

So the two heterozygous lanes (T/C, lanes 2 and 6) each show all three bands;
the two T/T homozygotes (lanes 3 and 5) show the control band plus the 194 bp
band only, with a visibly empty gap at the 313 bp position; the single C/C
homozygote (lane 4) shows the control band plus the 313 bp band only, with an
empty gap at the 194 bp position. The **456 bp control band is present in every
sample lane** and is the strongest, sharpest band in each.

Other features: all five sample lanes show loading wells as pale ovals at the
very top, a diffuse smear running from the well down through the high-molecular-
weight region, and a broad, very dark **primer-dimer / low-molecular-weight
band below the 100 bp marker** at the bottom of each sample lane. No numbers
other than the marker sizes and the three band sizes appear in the image.

## p003-x105.png

**Photograph (agarose gel, 7 lanes — an unlabelled ladder lane plus 6 samples).**

**Lane headings, left to right:** the left-most lane is the DNA size marker and
carries **no heading**; the six labelled sample lanes read **G/G**, **G/T**,
**T/T**, **G/T**, **G/T**, **T/T**.

**Size-marker labels printed at the left edge (top to bottom):** **500**,
**400**, **300**, **200**, **100**. The ladder lane shows an isolated faint
doublet near the top, then a stack of closely spaced rungs, then the labelled
500, 400, 300, 200 and 100 rungs.

**Band identity labels printed at the right edge, each with a horizontal arrow
pointing left into the gel (top to bottom):**
- **348 bp (control band)** — aligned between the 400 and 300 markers, nearer
  400.
- **281 bp (T allele)** — aligned just below the 300 marker.
- **151 bp (G allele)** — aligned between the 200 and 100 markers.

**Band pattern per lane** (✓ = present, — = absent):

| Lane | Label | 348 bp control | 281 bp (T allele) | 151 bp (G allele) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | (marker, unlabelled) | ladder | ladder | ladder |
| 2 | G/G | ✓ | — | ✓ |
| 3 | G/T | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| 4 | T/T | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| 5 | G/T | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| 6 | G/T | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| 7 | T/T | ✓ | ✓ | — |

The three heterozygous lanes (G/T — lanes 3, 5 and 6) each show all three bands.
The single G/G homozygote (lane 2) shows the 348 bp control and the 151 bp
G-allele band with a clearly empty gap at the 281 bp position. The two T/T
homozygotes (lanes 4 and 7) show the 348 bp control and the 281 bp T-allele band
with a clearly empty gap at the 151 bp position. The **348 bp control band is
present in all six sample lanes**.

Other features: loading wells visible as dark slots at the top of each sample
lane with vertical smearing beneath them; every sample lane ends in a broad dark
**primer-dimer smear below the 100 bp marker**, noticeably heavier in the lanes
that carry a 151 bp band (lanes 2, 3, 5, 6) than in the T/T lanes (4 and 7). No
numbers other than the marker sizes and the three band sizes appear in the
image.

---

### Relationship between the two gels

They are two different assays, not two runs of the same one: the first uses a
**456 bp control band with 313 bp (C) and 194 bp (T)** allele products and a
T/C polymorphism; the second uses a **348 bp control band with 281 bp (T) and
151 bp (G)** allele products and a G/T polymorphism. Both use the same ladder
labelling (500/400/300/200/100) and the same three-band ARMS-PCR read-out
convention.
