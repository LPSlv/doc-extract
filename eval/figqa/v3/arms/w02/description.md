**Figure (heatmap, p16).** A rectangular pixel/cell heatmap, cropped to the plot area only: the image contains the black plot frame and the coloured cells and nothing else. There is **no title, no axis labels, no tick marks, no tick values, no legend and no colour bar** anywhere in the image, so the quantities on either axis and the value scale of the colours cannot be read from it.

Grid: approximately 20 columns × 20 rows of uniform rectangular cells (cell width ≈ 22–23 px, height ≈ 16 px in a 457 × 324 px image).

Overlay: a black dashed vertical line and a black dashed horizontal line cross inside the plot, and a white five-pointed star marker sits exactly at their intersection. The intersection is at roughly 39% of the plot width from the left edge and roughly 21% of the plot height from the top edge — i.e. about eight cells in from the left and about four cells down from the top. Both dashed lines run the full width/height of the plot. No numeric annotation accompanies the star or the dashed lines.

Colour scale: a perceptually-uniform dark-to-bright ramp of the magma/inferno family — near-black and deep indigo at the low end, through purple and magenta, to red-orange and pale cream at the high end.

Pattern of values, read from the colours:

- The left-hand columns (roughly the first two) are uniformly deep purple (low) top to bottom.
- Values increase from left to right across most of the width, reaching their maximum around columns 11–17 (0-indexed 10–16).
- The brightest region is in the top rows. The single brightest cell is pale cream/near-white, located in the second row from the top, about 78% of the way across (column 16 of 20). Adjacent cells in the top two rows around columns 11–17 are bright orange/salmon.
- Brightness falls off downward: below roughly the top quarter, the whole field settles into medium magenta/purple, and the bottom half of the map is nearly row-invariant — each row repeats essentially the same left-to-right profile.
- A distinct **near-black block occupies the top-right corner**: the two right-most columns for roughly the top eight rows are the darkest cells in the whole image (one cell is essentially pure black), sharply discontinuous with the bright orange cells immediately to their left. Below that block, the same two right-most columns return to ordinary purple values comparable to the left of the map.
