**Figure set (six heatmap panels, p16).** Six separate raster panels, each 457 x 324 px, drawn in an identical style: a thin solid black rectangular frame enclosing a pixellated 2-D heatmap of roughly **20 columns x 20 rows** of square cells (cell pitch ~22.8 px horizontally, ~16.2 px vertically). Every panel carries one **horizontal black dashed line** and one **vertical black dashed line** that cross at a **white five-pointed star** marker. The colour scale is a perceptually-uniform dark-to-light map of the magma/inferno family: black -> dark navy -> purple -> magenta/pink -> orange -> pale cream, with pale cream the high end and black the low end.

**No panel contains any legible text.** There are no axis labels, no tick values, no titles, no legends, no colour bar and no printed numbers anywhere in any of the six images. All positions below are therefore given as fractions of the panel (x measured from the left edge, y measured from the **top** edge) and, where useful, in cell counts out of 20. Cell values are reported as relative luminance on a 0-255 scale (255 = brightest/pale cream, 0 = black), read from the pixels; they are not absolute data values, because no scale is printed.

The three crosshair positions repeat across the set, so the six panels form three pairs sharing a crosshair location:

## p016-x925.png

Heatmap, 457 x 324 px, 20 x 20 cells. Crosshair: vertical dashed line at x = 0.39, horizontal dashed line at y = 0.23 from the top, white star at their intersection.

Structure: a bright, hot band occupying the **top ~15 % of the panel** across the centre-right, over an otherwise flat, dull purple field that fills the lower two-thirds. Strong **vertical banding** — the field varies far more along x than along y in the lower part.

- Brightest cell: **row 1, column 15 of 20** — centre at x = 0.78, y = 0.07 from the top; luminance 210 (pale orange/cream). It sits inside a small pale patch in the upper-right quadrant.
- Darkest cell: **row 1, column 18** — x = 0.93, y = 0.07; luminance 1 (pure black). A black/near-black rectangular block occupies roughly x = 0.88-0.97, y = 0.00-0.13, i.e. immediately to the right of the bright patch. The bright maximum and the black minimum are therefore only about three cells apart on the same row.
- Column profile (mean luminance per column, left to right, 20 values): 53, 53, 59, 69, 74, 81, 91, 101, 99, 95, 106, 104, 101, 97, 97, 84, 85, 74, 37, 38. Rises from the dull left edge to a broad plateau around columns 7-15, then falls sharply; the last two columns (x > 0.90) are the darkest in the panel.
- Row profile (mean luminance per row, top to bottom): 98, 101, 87, 86, 87, 84, 81, 78, 80, 80, 77, 77, 77, 76, 74, 73, 71, 70, 70, 70. Brightest in the top two rows, then a slow monotonic decline downwards.
- The star sits in the magenta/pink zone just below the hot top band.
