**Figure (mechanical drawing — solder-paste stencil pattern). "SOLDER PASTE EXAMPLE",
"SCALE: 15X".**
Plan view of an 8-pin stencil aperture pattern: two vertical columns of four
red-outlined rectangular apertures each, left column and right column, separated by a
vertical dash-dot centreline labelled "SYMM" (with the centreline ℄ symbol) at the top.
A horizontal dash-dot centreline, also labelled "SYMM" with a ℄ symbol at its right
end, runs between the second and third apertures of each column, so the pattern is
symmetric about both axes. Each aperture is a rectangle wider than tall, with rounded
corners. Every aperture has its own short vertical dash-dot centre mark.

Pin-number callouts placed beside the apertures: "1" at the top-left aperture,
"4" at the bottom-left aperture, "8" at the top-right aperture, "5" at the
bottom-right aperture. Numbering therefore runs 1–4 top-to-bottom on the left column
and 8–5 top-to-bottom on the right column.

Dimension callouts (all values in parentheses, i.e. reference dimensions; units are
millimetres per the sheet title):
- "8X (1.4)" — horizontal dimension across the width of the top-left aperture,
  applied 8 times (aperture length).
- "8X (0.45)" — vertical dimension across the height of the top-left aperture,
  applied 8 times (aperture width).
- "6X (0.65)" — vertical dimension between the centrelines of two adjacent lower-left
  apertures, applied 6 times (aperture pitch).
- "(4.4)" — horizontal dimension, drawn below the pattern, between the vertical centre
  marks of the left and right columns (column-to-column span).
- "(R0.05) TYP" — leader line pointing to the rounded top-right corner of the
  top-right aperture; typical corner radius.

Inside the drawing border, bottom right: drawing number "4214862/A  04/2023".
