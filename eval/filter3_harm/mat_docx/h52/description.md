**Figure (geometric/domain schematic, p6).** *FIGURE 2. Parts of the interface between
$\Omega^{(1)}_{\varepsilon}$ and the thin fracture $\mathcal{R}_{\varepsilon}$.* The
page's only artwork, centred at the top under the running head.

Shape. A single large "squircle" — a square with strongly rounded corners, drawn with
a thin outline and filled pale pink/peach. It is the domain
$\Omega^{(1)}_{\varepsilon}$; the label $\Omega^{(1)}$ is set in the upper-left
interior of the pink region.

Inside the pink region sits a **Y-shaped teal band** — the thin fracture
$\mathcal{R}_{\varepsilon}$, drawn as a solid, appreciably thick strip (thin fracture
of width $\sim\varepsilon$, drawn wide enough to be visible). Its geometry:

- A roughly horizontal arm entering from the **left**, running rightwards at about
  mid-height of the squircle.
- A **node** (the junction $\mathcal{R}^{(0)}_{\varepsilon}$) at roughly the centre of
  the region, slightly right of and below centre, where the band forks into two.
- One arm from the node running **up and to the right**, meeting the boundary in the
  upper-right quadrant.
- One arm from the node running **right and slightly downward**, meeting the boundary
  at the right side.

The three arms leave the drawn region at three separate points on the squircle
outline, so the pink domain is cut by the fracture into connected pieces around it.

Legible strings inside the figure (all italic Greek/Roman with super/subscripts):

- $\Omega^{(1)}$ — upper-left, inside the pink region.
- $\Gamma^{(1,1)}$ — left of centre, above the left-hand horizontal arm.
- $\Gamma^{(1,0)}$ — just left of / above the node, labelling the interface piece at
  the junction itself.
- $\Gamma^{(1,2)}$ — upper right, labelling the interface along the up-right arm.

No numeric dimension callouts, no axes, no legend. Two isolated dot-sized marks sit
outside the pink region (one just above it, one to its right); they carry no label and
appear to be stray rule/period artefacts rather than content.

Relation to the surrounding text (for orientation only — the equations themselves are
extractable text): equation (2.3) states
$\partial\Omega^{(1)}_{\varepsilon}\cap\partial\mathcal{R}_{\varepsilon}
= \Gamma^{(1,1)}_{\varepsilon}\cup\Gamma^{(1,0)}_{\varepsilon}\cup\Gamma^{(1,2)}_{\varepsilon}$,
i.e. exactly the three labelled pieces drawn in the figure; the first index $i$ in
$\Gamma^{(i,j)}_{\varepsilon}$ identifies the domain $\partial\Omega^{(i)}_{\varepsilon}$
and the second index $j$ identifies the side of rectangle
$\mathcal{R}^{(j)}_{\varepsilon}$ when $j\neq 0$, or the part of the node boundary
$\mathcal{R}^{(0)}_{\varepsilon}$ when $j=0$.
