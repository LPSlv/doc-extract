## Figure 1 — land pattern example, NS0016A (16-pin SOP)

Titles beneath the artwork:

> **LAND PATTERN EXAMPLE**
> SCALE:7X

**Geometry.** Two vertical columns of eight blue-outlined rectangular lands each — 16 lands total. The left column is numbered `1` at the top and `8` at the bottom; the right column is numbered `16` at the top and `9` at the bottom. Each column is crossed by a vertical dash-dot centreline; a vertical "SYMM ℄" line runs between the columns and a horizontal "SYMM ℄" line crosses at mid-height.

**Dimension callouts, as printed:**

- `16X (1.85)` — land length, dimensioned horizontally at the top left; applies to all 16.
- `16X (0.6)` — land width, dimensioned vertically at the upper left; applies to all 16.
- `14X (1.27)` — land pitch, dimensioned vertically at the left between two adjacent lands (14 spaces for the 8-per-side rows).
- `(7)` — horizontal distance between the two column centrelines, dimensioned across the bottom.
- `(R0.05) TYP` — leader from the bottom-left land, giving the land corner radius.
- `SEE DETAILS` — leader from the top-right land, pointing at the solder-mask detail figure below.

All dimensions are in millimetres, shown in parentheses (reference dimensions).

## Figure 2 — solder mask details

Title beneath the pair: "**SOLDER MASK DETAILS**". Two enlarged single-pad views side by side.

**Left view — "NON SOLDER MASK DEFINED".** A rounded rectangular pad drawn with a green metal outline inside a blue dashed solder-mask boundary. Leaders: "METAL" (to the pad edge, from the left) and "SOLDER MASK OPENING" (to the dashed boundary, from above). Dimension across the gap between the metal edge and the mask edge: `0.07 MAX ALL AROUND`.

**Right view — "SOLDER MASK DEFINED".** Same pad geometry with the roles swapped: the solder mask opening is smaller than the metal. Leaders: "SOLDER MASK OPENING" (from above left) and "METAL" (from above right). Dimension across the overlap: `0.07 MIN ALL AROUND`.

**Notes below the frame:**

> NOTES: (continued)
> 5. Publication IPC-7351 may have alternate designs.
> 6. Solder mask tolerances between and around signal pads can vary based on board fabrication site.

Drawing number in the lower right of the frame: `4220735/A  12/2021`.
