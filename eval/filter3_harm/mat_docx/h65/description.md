Texas Instruments package drawing sheet. Page headings: "EXAMPLE BOARD LAYOUT"
(top right), "DRL0005A" (top left), "SOT - 0.6 mm max height" (top right, second
line), and beneath it "PLASTIC SMALL OUTLINE".
The sheet holds two drawings inside one full-page rectangular border.

**Figure 1 (mechanical drawing — PCB land pattern). "LAND PATTERN EXAMPLE",
"SCALE:30X".**
Plan view of a 5-pin land pattern: three blue-outlined rounded rectangular pads in a
left column and two in a right column. Each pad is wider than tall with rounded
corners. A vertical dash-dot centreline labelled "SYMM" (with a ℄ centreline symbol)
runs between the two columns; a horizontal dash-dot centreline, labelled "SYMM" with
a ℄ symbol at its right end, runs through the middle-left pad, and the right column
has no pad on that axis — the right column has pads only above and below it, so the
left column carries 3 pads and the right column 2, total 5.

Pin-number callouts: "1" at the top-left pad, "3" at the bottom-left pad,
"5" at the top-right pad, "4" at the bottom-right pad. (The middle-left pad is
unnumbered on the drawing; by sequence it is pin 2.)

Dimension callouts (values in parentheses are reference dimensions; units millimetres):
- "5X (0.67)" — horizontal dimension across the width of the top-left pad, 5 places
  (pad length).
- "5X (0.3)" — vertical dimension across the height of the top-left pad, 5 places
  (pad width).
- "2X (0.5)" — vertical dimension between the horizontal SYMM centreline and the
  centreline of the bottom-left pad, 2 places (pad pitch).
- "(1.48)" — horizontal dimension below the pattern, between the vertical centre
  marks of the left and right columns.
- "(1)" — vertical dimension at the far right, between the centrelines of the top-right
  and bottom-right pads.
- "(R0.05) TYP" — leader line pointing to the lower-left rounded corner of the
  bottom-left pad; typical corner radius.

**Figure 2 (mechanical detail — two side-by-side pad cross-section/plan details).
"SOLDERMASK DETAILS".**
Two rounded-rectangle pad details drawn at the bottom of the sheet.

Left detail — caption below it: "NON SOLDER MASK DEFINED (PREFERRED)".
A green outer rounded rectangle (the solder mask opening) enclosing a slightly smaller
blue rounded rectangle (the metal pad). Leader-line labels: "SOLDER MASK OPENING"
pointing to the green outline, "METAL" pointing to the blue outline. A dimension
callout at the upper left, "0.05 MAX AROUND", with an arrowed gap dimension pointing
at the clearance between the metal edge and the mask edge.

Right detail — caption below it: "SOLDER MASK DEFINED".
A blue dashed outer rounded rectangle (here the pad metal, drawn dashed because it is
under the mask) enclosing a green rounded rectangle (the solder mask opening).
Leader-line labels: "METAL UNDER SOLDER MASK" pointing to the inner/left edge and
"SOLDER MASK OPENING" pointing to the green outline. Dimension callout at the upper
left, "0.05 MIN AROUND", with an arrowed gap dimension on the clearance.

Inside the drawing border, bottom right: drawing number "4220753/E  11/2024".
