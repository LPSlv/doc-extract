## Figure 14 — S/N versus survey configuration (four-panel line chart)

**Type.** Four panels side by side in one row, sharing a single log y-axis; four
line series repeated in every panel.

- **Y axis** (labelled once, on the far-left panel): **S/N**, logarithmic, from
  $10^{-2}$ to $10^{3}$, decade ticks $10^{-2}, 10^{-1}, 10^{0}, 10^{1}, 10^{2},
  10^{3}$. A **grey shaded band fills the region S/N < 1** in every panel — the
  "undetected" zone.
- **Series / legend** (legend sits inside the left-hand panel):
  - "Hα×HeII" — blue
  - "Hα" — green
  - "HeII" — red
  - second legend block headed "**z**": **solid = 5**, **dashed = 12**.
  So each panel carries six curves: three signals x two redshifts.
- **Vertical reference lines.** Each panel carries vertical lines marking the value
  of that parameter for the three survey configurations, per the caption:
  **SPHEREx = dotted, CDIM = solid, CDIM+ = dashed**. In the second panel these three
  lines are labelled in the plot with rotated text reading "SPHEREx" (dotted, at low
  R), "CDIM" (solid, near R ≈ 300 — read from axis) and "CDIM+" (dashed, near
  R ≈ 500 — read from axis).

**Panel 1 (leftmost).** X: $\sigma_{\mathrm{noise}}$ [erg s$^{-1}$ cm$^{-2}$
sr$^{-1}$ Hz$^{-1}$], logarithmic, $10^{-21}$ to $10^{-15}$, ticks at $10^{-21}$,
$10^{-19}$, $10^{-17}$, $10^{-15}$. All six curves fall steeply and monotonically
with increasing noise. At the left edge ($10^{-21}$) the curves start at roughly
$10^{2}$–$3\times10^{2}$ for the strongest and around $10^{1}$ for the weakest (read
from axis); the green Hα $z=5$ curve is the last to cross out of the shaded band, at
about $\sigma_{\mathrm{noise}}\approx 10^{-17}$ (read from axis), while the red HeII
curves drop below S/N = 1 by about $10^{-20}$ (read from axis). Vertical
configuration lines cluster between roughly $10^{-20.5}$ and $10^{-19}$.

**Panel 2.** X: **spectral resolution R**, linear, ticks at 200 and 400 (axis runs
from near 0 to roughly 550, read from axis). All curves **rise** with R and flatten
toward the right. At the right edge the green Hα $z=5$ curve reaches roughly
$3\times10^{1}$, the green $z=12$ dashed curve roughly $10^{1}$, blue Hα×HeII $z=5$
roughly a few, and the red HeII curves sit at or just below S/N ≈ 1 (all read from
axis).

**Panel 3.** X: **pixel size ["]** (arcseconds), ticks at 5 and 10 (axis runs from
about 1 to about 11, read from axis). Curves **fall** with increasing pixel size:
green stays well above the shaded band across the range (falling from ~$10^{2}$
toward ~$3\times10^{1}$), blue starts near S/N ≈ 1 at small pixels and sinks into the
shaded band, red is inside the shaded band across most of the range (all read from
axis).

**Panel 4 (rightmost).** X: $\Omega_{\mathrm{survey}}$ [deg$^2$], ticks at 100, 200,
300. Curves **rise** with survey area and flatten. A vertical dotted reference line
sits at about 200 deg$^2$ (read from axis). At 300 deg$^2$: green $z=5$ ≈ $10^{2}$,
green $z=12$ ≈ $3\times10^{1}$, blue $z=5$ ≈ 5, blue $z=12$ ≈ 2, red $z=5$ ≈ 1, red
$z=12$ ≈ 0.5 (all read from axis).

**Caption (as printed).** "FIG. 14. **The current CDIM configuration would enable a
marginal detection of the Hα×HeII cross-correlation signal, and a more sophisticated
instrument or deeper integration time are necessary for a higher significance
detection of top-heavy star formation.** The cumulative S/N ratio predicted for a
range of survey configurations based on our fiducial model and IMF applied for each of
the three LIM signals discussed in this work at z = 5 and 12 (solid and dashed lines,
respectively). From left to right, the four panels vary the instrument sensitivity,
spectral resolution, pixel size, and survey area individually holding the other
parameters fixed at the current CDIM specifications summarized in Table I. The
vertical lines in each panel correspond to the value of that parameter for each of the
survey configurations explored in this work (SPHEREx is dotted, CDIM is solid, and
CDIM+ is dashed)."

## Figure 15 — Pop III / Pop II UV luminosity functions (six-panel line chart)

**Type.** 3 columns x 2 rows of panels, each showing UV luminosity functions; every
panel repeats the same fiducial pair in black and adds a coloured family of curves for
one varied parameter, with an inset horizontal colourbar giving that parameter's
range.

- **Y axis** (labelled on the left column of both rows): $\Phi_{\mathrm{UV}}$
  [Mpc$^{-3}$ mag$^{-1}$], logarithmic, $10^{-6}$ to $10^{0}$, ticks at $10^{-6}$,
  $10^{-4}$, $10^{-2}$, $10^{0}$.
- **X axis** (labelled on the bottom row of all three columns): $M_{\mathrm{UV}}$,
  linear and running **more negative to the right**, ticks at $-10$, $-15$, $-20$
  (axis extends slightly past $-20$).
- **Black curves in every panel:** **solid = pop III**, **dashed = pop II** — the
  fiducial model used for the Fisher forecasts of Fig. 12. The solid black pop III
  curve starts near $\Phi_{\mathrm{UV}}\approx 10^{0}$ at $M_{\mathrm{UV}}=-10$ and
  cuts off steeply near $M_{\mathrm{UV}}\approx-13$ to $-14$; the dashed black pop II
  curve declines much more gradually and extends past $M_{\mathrm{UV}}=-20$ (read from
  axis).
- **Legend** (inside the lower-right panel): "pop III" solid, "pop II" dashed.
- **Annotation** "$z = 5$" in the top-right corner of the upper-right panel.
