**Fig. 12. Package outline SOT360-1 (TSSOP20)**

Type: mechanical package outline drawing (four views plus a detail, a scale bar,
a dimension table and a references table), all inside one ruled frame.

Frame header line: left "TSSOP20: plastic thin shrink small outline package;
20 leads; body width 4.4 mm", right "SOT360-1".

### Views

**Top-left — front elevation.** The moulded body seen from the long side, with
ten gull-wing leads visible below it and a small dashed line along the top
surface. Dimension **D** spans the full body length with double-headed arrows
across the top. Below the leads, a datum/flatness feature control frame is drawn:
a parallelogram (flatness/coplanarity symbol) box followed by a box containing
**y**, attached by a leader to the seating plane.

**Top-right — side elevation.** The body seen end-on, leads bent out to left and
right. Dimensions:
- **E** — body width, arrows across the top of the body, with the right-hand
  extension line tied to a boxed datum **A**.
- **H_E** — overall width including the leads, arrows across the bottom.
- **c** — lead thickness, a small vertical dimension at the left-hand lead.
- A dashed circle at the right-hand lead is called out as detail **X** by a leader
  to a boxed **X**.
- Feature control frame at the lower right: a symmetry symbol (three stacked bars),
  then **v**, then **Ⓜ** (maximum material condition), then datum **A**.

**Bottom-left — plan (bottom/land) view.** The rectangular body with ten leads on
the top edge and ten on the bottom edge. Corner pin numbers are printed at the
four corners of the lead field: **20** top-left, **11** top-right, **1** bottom-left,
**10** bottom-right. A circle inside the body at the lower left is annotated by an
arrow with the text "pin 1 index". Horizontal and vertical dash-dot centrelines
cross the body. Dimensions:
- **Z** — a short dimension at the top-left between the body edge and the first lead.
- **e** — lead pitch, boxed label under a dimension between two adjacent lower leads.
- **b_p** — lead width, dimension on a lower lead.
- Feature control frame beside b_p: position symbol (⊕), then **w**, then **Ⓜ**.

**Bottom-right — "detail X"** (boxed label). Enlarged cross-section of one
gull-wing lead leaving the body. Dimensions:
- **A** — overall height, full-height dimension at the right.
- **A₂** — body thickness, full-height dimension at the left.
- **A₁** — stand-off, small dimension at the left just above the seating plane.
- **(A₃)** — reference dimension (parenthesised), from the lead top surface.
- **Q** — vertical dimension from the top of the drawing down to the lead shoulder.
- **L** — overall lead length (foot span), lower dimension.
- **L_p** — foot length, shorter dimension above L.
- **θ** — lead exit angle, angular callout at the foot.

**Scale bar** (centred, below the views): a ruler graduated `0`, `2.5`, `5 mm`,
labelled "scale".
