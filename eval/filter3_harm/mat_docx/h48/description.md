**Figure 1** — CONSORT-style flow chart of participant enrolment and allocation.
Caption printed beneath it: *"Flow chart for enrolment and allocation of
students with refractive error in a randomised trial of free eyeglasses to
promote eyeglasses wear."*

The chart runs top to bottom, with four shaded stage labels down the centre
(`Enrolment`, `Allocation`, `Follow-up`, `Analysis`) and plain rectangular boxes
carrying the counts. Arrows as described.

1. Top box: `Total Population` / `31 schools, 2095 students`.
2. A diagonal arrow leads right from the top box to an exclusion box containing
   two bullets:
   - `979 students excluded (46.7%): not having visual acuity ≤ 6/12 in either eye`
   - `121 students excluded (5.8%): new students after baseline`
3. Stage label `Enrolment` (shaded), then a downward arrow to:
   `Randomised` / `995 students (47.5%) at 31 schools`.
4. Downward arrow to the stage label `Allocation` (shaded); the flow then splits
   left and right into two allocation boxes:
   - Left: `Intervention Group: Free Glasses` / `515 students (51.8%) at 15 schools`
   - Right: `Control Group: Glasses prescription only 480 students (48.2%) at 16 schools`
5. Stage label `Follow-up` (shaded) in the centre. From each allocation arm a
   horizontal arrow points outward to a loss-to-follow-up box:
   - Left: `Lost to follow-up: 39 students (7.6%) from Intervention Group`
   - Right: `Lost to follow-up: 46 students (9.6%) from Control Group`
6. Stage label `Analysis` (shaded) in the centre, then each arm continues
   downward to an analysis box:
   - Left: `Analysed: 476 students (52.3%) at 15 schools`
   - Right: `Analysed: 434 students (47.7%) at 16 schools`
