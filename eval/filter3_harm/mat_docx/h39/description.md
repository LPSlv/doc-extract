Page heading: `EXAMPLE BOARD LAYOUT`, package `DDA0008B`,
`PowerPAD™ SOIC - 1.7 mm max height`, sub-heading `PLASTIC SMALL OUTLINE`.
All drawings sit inside one ruled frame. Texas Instruments logo and www.ti.com
at the foot of the page.

**Figure 1 — land pattern drawing (top, captioned `LAND PATTERN EXAMPLE`,
`SCALE:10X`).**

Plan view of a PCB land pattern for an 8-pin small-outline package with a
central thermal pad. Eight rounded-rectangle copper pads in two vertical
columns of four (left column numbered `1` at the top to `4` at the bottom;
right column numbered `8` at the top to `5` at the bottom). Between the columns
is a square central thermal land numbered `9`, drawn in green (solder-mask
defined) with a dashed grey outline around it showing the solder mask opening,
and four small vias (circles with cross-hairs) inside it. Horizontal and
vertical `SYMM` centre-lines (with the centreline symbol ℄) run through the
pattern.

Dimension callouts, all in parentheses (reference dimensions), millimetres:

- `(2.95) NOTE 9` — overall width across the dashed mask region, topmost
  dimension.
- `(2.71) SOLDER MASK OPENING` — width of the central solder mask opening.
- `8X (1.55)` — length of each of the eight perimeter pads.
- `8X (0.6)` — width of each of the eight perimeter pads.
- `6X (1.27)` — pad pitch (six spaces of 1.27).
- `(R0.05) TYP` — pad corner radius.
- `(⌀0.2) TYP VIA` — via diameter, leader pointing to a via in the thermal pad.
- `(1.3) TYP` — via-to-via spacing, given twice: once horizontally at the bottom
  (`(1.3) TYP`) and once vertically at the right of the thermal pad
  (`(1.3) TYP`).
- `(5.4)` — overall pattern width, bottom-most dimension.
- `(3.4) SOLDER MASK OPENING` — vertical extent of the solder mask opening.
- `(4.9) NOTE 9` — overall vertical dimension at the far right.

Leader-line callouts: `SOLDER MASK DEFINED PAD` (points to the central pad 9),
`SEE DETAILS` (points to a perimeter pad in the right column), `METAL COVERED BY
SOLDER MASK` (points to the region between the perimeter pads and the central
pad), `SOLDER MASK OPENING` (twice, as part of the dimensions above), `SYMM`
(both centre-lines).

**Figure 2 — solder mask details (bottom, captioned `SOLDER MASK DETAILS`,
`PADS 1-8`).** Two side-by-side detail views of a single rounded-rectangular
pad, each drawn at large scale.

- Left detail: dimension `0.07 MAX ALL AROUND` across the gap between the mask
  edge and the metal. The outer rounded rectangle is the solder mask opening
  (labelled `SOLDER MASK OPENING`, leader to the outline) and the inner green
  rounded rectangle is the copper (labelled `METAL`). Caption beneath:
  `NON SOLDER MASK DEFINED`.
- Right detail: dimension `0.07 MIN ALL AROUND`. Labels `SOLDER MASK OPENING`
  (leader to the inner solid outline) and `METAL UNDER SOLDER MASK` (leader to
  the outer dashed blue outline). Caption beneath: `SOLDER MASK DEFINED`.

Drawing number in the lower right of the frame: `4214849/B  09/2025`.
