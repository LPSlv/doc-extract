## Figure — land pattern example, DPW0005A (5-pin X2SON)

One plan-view drawing inside a rectangular frame. Titles beneath the artwork:

> **LAND PATTERN EXAMPLE**
> SOLDER MASK DEFINED
> SCALE:60X

**Geometry.** Five lands. Four are corner pads arranged two-up / two-down about the crossed symmetry centrelines: pad `1` top left, `5` top right, `2` bottom left, `4` bottom right. Each corner pad is drawn as a green metal outline (a rounded rectangle with one chamfered/angled inner corner facing the centre) sitting inside a larger blue dashed solder-mask outline. The fifth land, `3`, is a small diamond/rounded-square pad at the exact centre of the pattern, also with a blue dashed mask outline around it, and it carries a via at its centre.

Two dash-dot symmetry centrelines cross at the middle of the pattern; both are labelled "SYMM ℄" — one at the top (vertical) and one at the left (horizontal).

**Dimension and note callouts, as printed:**

- `(0.78)` — overall horizontal span, dimensioned across the top between the outer edges of the left and right pad groups.
- `4X (0.42)` — pad length, dimensioned horizontally at the top left; applies to the four corner pads.
- `4X (0.22)` — pad width, dimensioned vertically at the left; applies to the four corner pads.
- `4X (0.26)` — horizontal dimension on the right-hand side, from the pattern centre out to the corner pads.
- `(0.48)` — vertical dimension at the far right, between the centres of the upper and lower right-hand pads.
- `4X (0.06)` — leader/dimension at the lower right, to a small feature on the bottom-right pad.
- `(⌀ 0.1) VIA` — leader to the circle at the centre of pad 3, giving the via drill/finished diameter.
- `0.05 MIN ALL AROUND TYP` — dimension at the top right, the minimum mask-over-metal overlap.
- `(R0.05) TYP` — leader at the lower left, to pad 2, giving the pad corner radius.
- `(▫0.25)` — leader at the lower left, a square-symbol dimension (nominal 0.25 square) to the centre pad region.
- `(0.21) TYP EXPOSED METAL CLEARANCE` — leader from the centre of the pattern.
- `SOLDER MASK OPENING, TYP` — leader at the lower right, to the blue dashed outline.
- `METAL UNDER SOLDER MASK TYP` — leader at the lower right, to the green metal outline where it runs under the mask.

All dimensions are in millimetres; the parenthesised ones are reference dimensions.
