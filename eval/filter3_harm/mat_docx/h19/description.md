Text above the frame: "XSON6: extremely thin small outline package; no leads; 6 terminals; body 1.0 x 1.0 x 0.35 mm" (left) and "SOT1202" (right).

Caption below the frame: "Fig. 16. Package outline SOT1202 (XSON6)".

**Figure (mechanical package outline drawing, three orthographic views plus a top view, European first-angle projection).**

### View 1 — bottom view (upper left of the frame)

A square package body outline containing six rectangular terminal pads laid out in two rows of three. Pads in the upper row are numbered **1, 2, 3** left to right (labels above the body); pads in the lower row are numbered **6, 5, 4** left to right (labels below the body). Terminal 1 (upper left) is drawn with a chamfered/cut lower-left corner, marking pin 1.

Dimension callouts on this view:

- **b** — horizontal dimension across the width of a terminal pad, at the top over pin 3.
- **L** — vertical dimension of the terminal pad length, at the right over pin 3.
- **L₁** — vertical dimension of the chamfered pin-1 pad length, at the left.
- **e** — boxed dimension symbol, vertical, giving the row-to-row pitch (between the upper and lower row centrelines).
- **e₁** — boxed dimension symbol, used twice along the bottom, giving the pad-to-pad pitch within a row (pin 6 to pin 5, and pin 5 to pin 4).

Dash-dot centrelines run through each pad column and horizontally through the package centre.

### View 2 — side view (upper right of the frame)

Narrow rectangular outline with two terminal pads visible on the near face and two on the far edge; a horizontal dash-dot centreline. Callout with leader line: "(4×)⁽²⁾" pointing at a terminal, i.e. 4 places, referencing note 2.

### View 3 — front / end view (middle of the frame)

Rectangular body outline in elevation with three terminals visible along the bottom face and their standoff pads beneath. Callout with leader line: "(6×)⁽²⁾" — 6 places, referencing note 2. Dimension callouts at the right:

- **A₁** — standoff height (from seating plane to the underside of the body).
- **A** — total package height.

### View 4 — top view (lower centre of the frame)

A square body outline with a horizontal and vertical dash-dot centreline. The lower-left quadrant is shaded grey and labelled with a leader line: "terminal 1 index area". Dimension callouts:

- **D** — horizontal, overall body length, above the view.
- **E** — vertical, overall body width, at the right of the view.

### Scale bar

Beneath the views: a graduated scale bar with tick labels "0", "0.5", "1 mm" and the word "scale" below it.
