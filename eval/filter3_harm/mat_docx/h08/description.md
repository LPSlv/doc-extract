## Figure — solder-paste stencil pattern, PW0014A (14-pin TSSOP)

A single plan-view drawing inside a rectangular frame. Titles beneath the artwork:

> **SOLDER PASTE EXAMPLE**
> BASED ON 0.125 mm THICK STENCIL
> SCALE: 10X

**Geometry.** Two vertical columns of seven red-outlined rectangular stencil apertures each — 14 apertures total. The left column is numbered `1` at the top and `7` at the bottom; the right column is numbered `14` at the top and `8` at the bottom (so numbering runs down the left column and up the right, the usual TSSOP order). Each column of apertures is crossed by a vertical grey dash-dot centreline; a vertical "SYMM" centreline runs between the two columns, and a horizontal "SYMM" centreline crosses at mid-height. Both symmetry lines carry the centreline symbol ℄.

**Dimension callouts, as printed:**

- `14X (1.5)` — aperture length, dimensioned horizontally across one aperture at the top left; applies to all 14.
- `14X (0.45)` — aperture width, dimensioned vertically at the top left; applies to all 14.
- `12X (0.65)` — pad pitch, dimensioned vertically at the left between two adjacent apertures (12 spaces for the 7-per-side rows).
- `(5.8)` — horizontal distance between the two column centrelines, dimensioned across the bottom.
- `(R0.05) TYP` — leader from the top-right aperture corner, giving the aperture corner radius.

All dimensions are in millimetres and shown in parentheses (reference dimensions).

**Notes below the frame:**

> NOTES: (continued)
> 8. Laser cutting apertures with trapezoidal walls and rounded corners may offer better paste release. IPC-7525 may have alternate design recommendations.
> 9. Board assembly site may have different recommendations for stencil design.

Drawing number in the lower right of the frame: `4220202/B  12/2023`.
