## Figure 1 — land pattern example, PW0016A (16-pin TSSOP)

Titles beneath the artwork:

> **LAND PATTERN EXAMPLE**
> EXPOSED METAL SHOWN
> SCALE: 10X

**Geometry.** Two vertical columns of eight blue-outlined rectangular lands each — 16 lands total. The left column is numbered `1` at the top and `8` at the bottom; the right column is numbered `16` at the top and `9` at the bottom. Each column is crossed by a vertical dash-dot centreline; a vertical "SYMM ℄" line runs between the columns and a horizontal "SYMM ℄" line crosses at mid-height.

**Dimension callouts, as printed:**

- `16X (1.5)` — land length, dimensioned horizontally at the top left; applies to all 16.
- `16X (0.45)` — land width, dimensioned vertically at the upper left; applies to all 16.
- `14X (0.65)` — land pitch, dimensioned vertically at the left between two adjacent lands (14 spaces for the 8-per-side rows).
- `(5.8)` — horizontal distance between the two column centrelines, dimensioned across the bottom.
- `(R0.05) TYP` — leader from the top-right land, giving the land corner radius.

All dimensions are in millimetres, shown in parentheses (reference dimensions).

## Figure 2 — solder mask details

Title beneath the pair: "**SOLDER MASK DETAILS**". Two enlarged single-pad views side by side.

**Left view — "NON-SOLDER MASK DEFINED (PREFERRED)".** A rectangular pad with a green metal outline inside a dashed solder-mask boundary. Leaders: "SOLDER MASK OPENING" (from upper left), "METAL" (from upper right), "EXPOSED METAL" (from the left, to the pad interior). Dimension across the gap between the metal edge and the mask edge: `0.05 MAX ALL AROUND`.

**Right view — "SOLDER MASK DEFINED".** Same pad with the roles swapped: the mask opening is smaller than the metal. Leaders: "METAL UNDER SOLDER MASK" (from upper left), "SOLDER MASK OPENING" (from upper right), "EXPOSED METAL" (from the right). Dimension across the overlap: `0.05 MIN ALL AROUND`.
