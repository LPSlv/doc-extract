## Figure 12 — Normalized Maximum Transient Thermal Impedance for AOW14N50 (Note F)

**Type:** log-log line chart, family of 7 curves.

- **X axis:** "Pulse Width (s)", logarithmic, decade labels 0.00001, 0.0001, 0.001, 0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100; dense minor log gridlines.
- **Y axis:** "Z_θJC Normalized Transient Thermal Resistance", logarithmic, decade labels 0.001, 0.01, 0.1, 1, 10.

**Text box, upper left inside the plot:**

- $D = T_{on}/T$
- $T_{J,PK} = T_C + P_{DM} \cdot Z_{\theta JC} \cdot R_{\theta JC}$
- $R_{\theta JC} = 0.45\ ^\circ\mathrm{C/W}$

**Text box, upper right inside the plot:** "In descending order" / "D=0.5, 0.3, 0.1, 0.05, 0.02, 0.01, single pulse"

**In-plot label:** "Single Pulse", with a leader to the lowest curve.

**Inset, lower right inside the plot:** a small waveform sketch of a rectangular pulse train (two pulses shown). Labels: "P_D" with a vertical arrow marking the pulse amplitude; "T_on" with a horizontal double arrow marking the pulse-on width; "T" with a horizontal double arrow marking the full period.

**Curve values (left-hand plateau, at pulse width 1 × 10⁻⁵ s), read from axis, top to bottom:**

| Curve (duty cycle D) | Z_θJC at 10 µs |
|---|---|
| D = 0.5 | ~0.5 |
| D = 0.3 | ~0.3 |
| D = 0.1 | ~0.10 |
| D = 0.05 | ~0.055 |

All seven curves rise monotonically with pulse width and converge onto a common asymptote of Z_θJC = 1 at roughly 0.05–0.1 s, remaining flat at 1 out to 100 s.
