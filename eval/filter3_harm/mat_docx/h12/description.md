## Figure 4-6 — package pinout diagram

Caption, two lines: "**Figure 4-6. TL072M U Package, 10-Pin CFP**" / "**(Top View)**". Note printed just under the package body: "Not to scale".

A top-view outline of a 10-pin ceramic flat package: a tall rounded rectangle for the body with five flat leads leaving each side. Inside the body, pin numbers are printed against each lead — `1` to `5` down the left side and `10` down to `6` on the right side (numbering runs down the left column and up the right). Each lead is drawn as an open rectangle running outward from the body, with a small pin-tail hatch mark where it meets the body.
