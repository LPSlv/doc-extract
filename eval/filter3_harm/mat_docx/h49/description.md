**Figure (flow chart, p73).** *Fig. 1. Flow chart for inclusion.* Patient-selection
cascade for a Taiwan National Health Insurance haemophilia study. Single vertical
chain of nine rounded boxes down the page, each connected to the next by a short
downward arrow; at four points a side branch leaves the chain to the right into an
exclusion box, and the count in that exclusion box is the number removed at that
step. All boxes are plain outlined rectangles with the counts written as `n=…`
inside.

Main chain, top to bottom:

1. "1997-2007 registration files for catastrophic illness patients — n=13285657"
2. "1997-2007 registration files for deficiency of clotting factors — n=20646"
3. "Registration files for deficiency of clotting factor — n=1401"
4. "Registration files for factor VIII or factor IX disorder patients — n=1041"
5. "Registration files for factor VIII or factor IX disorder male patients — n=988"

Side (exclusion) boxes, each on a right-hand branch, in the order they occur:

- after box 1: "Exclude not deficiency of clotting factors diagnosis patients — n=13265011"
- after box 2: "Patient-centered unique identifier review exclude duplication — n=19245"
- after box 3: "Exclude not factor VIII or factor IX disorder patients — n=360"
- after box 4: "Exclude factor VIII or factor IX disorder female patients — n=53"

Terminal split: box 5 branches into two side-by-side boxes on the bottom row —
left "Registration files for Haemophilia B male patients — n=155"; right
"Registration files for Haemophilia A male patients — n=833".

The arithmetic is internally consistent at every step: 13 285 657 − 13 265 011 =
20 646; 20 646 − 19 245 = 1 401; 1 401 − 360 = 1 041; 1 041 − 53 = 988; and
155 + 833 = 988.

No other figures on the page. Page furniture: running head "Factor Usage Trends of
Hemophiliacs in Taiwan"; footer "Yonsei Med J http://www.eymj.org Volume 54
Number 1 January 2013", page number 73.
