Page heading: `EXAMPLE BOARD LAYOUT`, package `DSK0010A`,
`WSON - 0.8 mm max height`, sub-heading `PLASTIC SMALL OUTLINE - NO LEAD`.
All drawings sit inside one ruled frame. Texas Instruments logo and www.ti.com
at the foot of the page.

**Figure 1 — land pattern drawing (top, captioned `LAND PATTERN EXAMPLE`,
`SCALE:20X`).**

Plan view of a PCB land pattern for a 10-pin WSON package with a central
thermal pad. Ten rounded-rectangle pads outlined in blue, arranged in two
vertical columns of five: left column numbered `1` at the top to `5` at the
bottom; right column numbered `6` at the bottom to `10` at the top. Between the
columns is a large blue-outlined central thermal land numbered `11`, containing
four vias (circles with cross-hairs) arranged at the top, centre-left,
centre-right and bottom. Horizontal and vertical `SYMM` centre-lines (with the
centreline symbol ℄) are marked, one below the pattern and one at the right.

Dimension callouts, all in parentheses (reference dimensions), millimetres:

- `10X (0.6)` — length of each of the ten perimeter pads.
- `10X (0.25)` — width of each of the ten perimeter pads.
- `8X (0.5)` — pad pitch (eight spaces of 0.5).
- `(R0.05) TYP` — pad corner radius.
- `(⌀0.2) VIA TYP` — via diameter, leader pointing to a via in the thermal pad.
- `(1.2)` — width of the central thermal land, dimensioned across the top.
- `(0.35)` — small dimension at the bottom of the central land (via/land offset).
- `(0.75)` — vertical dimension at the right of the pattern.
- `(2)` — overall vertical dimension, far right.
- `(2.3)` — overall pattern width, bottom-most dimension.

**Figure 2 — solder mask details (bottom, captioned `SOLDER MASK DETAILS`).**
Two side-by-side detail views of a single rounded-rectangular pad at large
scale.

- Left detail: dimension `0.07 MAX ALL AROUND`. Labels `SOLDER MASK OPENING`
  (leader to the outer outline) and `METAL` (leader to the inner green rounded
  rectangle). Caption beneath: `NON SOLDER MASK DEFINED (PREFERRED)`.
- Right detail: dimension `0.07 MIN ALL AROUND`. Labels `METAL UNDER SOLDER
  MASK` (leader to the outer dashed blue outline) and `SOLDER MASK OPENING`
  (leader to the inner outline). Caption beneath: `SOLDER MASK DEFINED`.

Drawing number in the lower right of the frame: `4218903/C  09/2025`.
