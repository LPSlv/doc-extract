## FIGURE 2. SIMPLE OUTPUT DEVICE LOAD LINE

Type: schematic line graph (conceptual, no numeric scale on either axis). Sits in
the right-hand column.

- X axis: output current, unscaled. Three tick marks only, labelled left to right
  **I_OUT,NL**, **I_OUT,MID**, **I_OUT,MAX**. No units printed.
- Y axis: output voltage, unscaled. Three tick marks only, labelled top to bottom
  **V_OUT,NL**, **V_OUT,NOM**, **V_OUT,FL**. No units printed.
- Thin grey dashed horizontal guide lines run from V_OUT,NL and V_OUT,FL across
  the plot; a grey dashed vertical guide line rises at I_OUT,MID.

Two data series:
- **NOMINAL LOAD LINE** — red, heavy dashed, perfectly horizontal, drawn at
  V_OUT,NOM across the whole current range. It is flat: output voltage independent
  of load current.
- **DROOP LOAD LINE** — magenta, heavy solid, a straight line of negative slope.
  It starts at (I_OUT,NL, V_OUT,NL) at the upper left, crosses the nominal line
  exactly at I_OUT,MID / V_OUT,NOM, and ends at (I_OUT,MAX, V_OUT,FL) at the
  lower right.

Legend, in a row beneath the plot: "NOMINAL LOAD LINE" with a red dashed swatch;
"DROOP LOAD LINE" with a magenta solid swatch. Colour here encodes which of the
two load lines is which.
